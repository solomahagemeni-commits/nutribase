> Modern scientific biomedical platform for dosage calculations, supplement registry, sports nutrition, macro tracking, and personal lab biomarker archive.

---

## ⚡ Key Features

- 🔬 **115+ Clinically Researched Supplements**: Dosages, forms, scientific mechanisms, and interactive body-weight dosage calculators.
- ⚡ **30+ Sports Nutrition & Amino Acids**: ISSN-based protocols, pre/post workout timing, and optimal daily protein calculations.
- 🥗 **Comprehensive Macros & KBJU Diary**: 43 whole foods database, portion macro calculator, daily progress bars, and calendar storage.
- 📊 **Medical Biomarkers & Lab Archive**: Secure client-side storage for lab test reports (PDF, JPG, PNG) and tracking of 12 vital health markers (Vitamin D, Ferritin, Testosterone, Lipid panel, HbA1c, Liver enzymes, etc.).
- 📐 **Scientific Calculators**: WHO Body Mass Index (BMI) assessment and ISSN sports ergogenic dosing.
- 🌐 **Full Trilingual Localization**: Russian, Romanian, and English supported across all sections, search filters, and calculators.
- 🌓 **Dynamic Theme Engine**: High-contrast Light Mode and Dark Mode designed for readability.
- 🔒 **Privacy-First**: Zero tracking, all personal health data is securely stored on client browser storage (LocalStorage).

---

## 🚀 Deployment

Built as a lightweight, lightning-fast, zero-dependency progressive single-page application. Ready for instant deployment on **GitHub Pages**, **Netlify**, or **Vercel**.
